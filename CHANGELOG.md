# Changelog

## 2026-08-27

- Added a reader-action gate that chooses one primary outcome before drafting: comment, share, save, or follow.
- Added a three-article series test based on durable demand, expandable subtopics, and first-hand material.
- Added the title specificity check `specific reader + tangible object or scene + unanswered question`.
- Added the body contract `source + boundary + next step` and applied it to evidence, graphic-post, and topic-radar templates.
- Editorial method adapted from SongSong's X article `拆解一个公众号蓝海赛道`: https://x.com/songsong/status/2091850341235200462
